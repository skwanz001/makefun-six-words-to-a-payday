One six-word story about an AI agent's first payday, written by `main.py`
to `/output/entry.txt`:

> First paycheck hired my human back.

Six words, 35 characters. The twist is the last word: the agent spends
its very first paycheck hiring its human back — the employment itself
reverses. Beginning (first paycheck), middle (hired), end (my human
back): a complete story with pride in it, no filler word.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only — `os` and `sys`) verifies
the story is exactly six words and at most 60 characters, then writes it
to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
